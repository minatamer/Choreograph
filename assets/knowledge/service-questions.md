# Service Questions

Juniper Cleaning - fictional demo company
Version 1.0 | Effective 22 October 2026

## Service area
This demonstration accepts fictional homes in the visitor's selected local timezone. It has no real dispatch area, postcode eligibility check or travel charge. Do not enter a real address. The coordinator view represents a fictional cleaning business.

## Supplies
Juniper brings ordinary products, cloths, a vacuum and a mop. Ask the coordinator before requesting a particular product or equipment change. The Service Guide and Safety and Exclusions describe the limits of those requests.

## Results and guarantees
The guides do not promise a landlord deposit return or a property inspection result. They describe the agreed cleaning scope. Ask for a coordinator review if a requirement goes beyond that scope.

## Booking and CRM
A submitted request is awaiting review until a coordinator approves it. HubSpot sharing is optional and separate from the reservation. Continuing without CRM creates no CRM records. This is a fictional demonstration; it does not send staff, emails or text messages.
