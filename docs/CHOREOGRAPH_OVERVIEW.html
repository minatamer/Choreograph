The full guest flow

A conversation that
gets something arranged.

Try Choreograph through fictional Juniper Cleaning, without an account. Explore a complete customer request, then see how the coordinator reviews and fulfils the booking. V1 focuses on this one company and service catalogue.

  1. Enter the fictional demo

    Try demo creates a private, expiring Juniper workspace. No Choreograph signup or document upload is required.

  2. Choose the CRM path

    Connect your own HubSpot or Continue without CRM. At connection capacity, keep the no-CRM path available.

  3. Describe the job

    Choose a ready-made job starter or question, then Send. Optional free typing stays available; no need to invent a prompt.

  4. Get a sourced answer

    The assistant consults current company guides. Read the cited page alongside its answer on desktop, then choose any missing booking details.

  5. Review the proposal

    Check the service, add-ons, exact catalogue price and duration. Policy text never sets the price.

  6. Request a time

    See the occupied week in chat, choose an eligible alternative in your local timezone and review it. It stays awaiting review until the business approves it.

  7. Confirm from the business side

    Review, assign and reserve. PostgreSQL prevents overlapping approvals.

  8. Keep records connected

    If you connected HubSpot, inspect the synced fictional record in your own CRM. Without CRM, the booking stays saved and no CRM sync runs.

  9. Handle changes

    Ask preparation questions, reschedule, cancel or complete a job, with clear review and sync states.

  10. Keep guidance current

    In Business → Documents, inspect or replace a guide. Once ready, new answers cite the new version; existing bookings stay intact.

Current evidence · Research and design rationale

The selected foundation

A guest experience.
A real backend.

Each visitor gets a private, expiring workspace. Server commands own exact prices, approvals and reservations; encrypted private objects persist the records without a database.

Optional visitor HubSpot OAuth remains independent of AI mode. Skipping CRM makes no HubSpot calls or CRM work.

No-database hosting

Three scale-to-zero Cloud Run services, a private GCS bucket and one authenticated minute Scheduler-driven private worker. Secret Manager holds server credentials. No Cloud SQL or always-running WorkerPool.
